# Nordic & Technical Color Palettes

Refined palettes combining Nordic minimalism with technical depth.

## Palette 1: Catppuccin Mocha (Warmer Technical)
- **Background**: `#1E1E2E` (Mocha Base)
- **Surface**: `#313244` (Surface0)
- **Accent**: `#89B4FA` (Blue) or `#F5C2E7` (Flamingo)
- **Muted Text**: `#6C7086` (Overlay0)
- **Highlight**: `#CDD6F4` (Text)

## Palette 2: Tokyo Night (Cooler Neutral)
- **Background**: `#1A1B26` (Storm)
- **Surface**: `#24283B` (Surface)
- **Accent**: `#7AA2F7` (Blue) or `#BB9AF7` (Purple)
- **Muted Text**: `#565F89` (Comment)
- **Highlight**: `#C0CAF5` (Text)

## Palette 4: Aether Frost (Light & Futuristic)
- **Background**: `rgba(242, 244, 248, 0.7)` (Frosted Glass Base)
- **Surface**: `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.4)` (Subtle Glass Layer)
- **Accent**: `#3A86FF` (High-Tech Blue)
- **Muted Text**: `#94A3B8` (Slate Technical Text)
- **Highlight**: `#1E293B` (Deep Contrast Text)
- **Border**: `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.8)` (Sharp light edges)
